An **Installment** captures one meaningful project moment as a curated snapshot.

Unlike a Journal entry, an installment is not written merely because work happened. Unlike a Changelog record, it is not limited to externally meaningful deltas. Unlike an Article, it is not primarily an essay or argument.

An installment asks:

> What should someone read to understand this particular moment in the project?

Each installment should therefore connect the records that already exist rather than recreate them.

## Installment anatomy

A strong installment normally identifies:

- the situation before the change,
- the problem or pressure that made the old arrangement insufficient,
- the important records produced during the work,
- the resulting state,
- what remains unresolved,
- where to continue reading.

Installments are curated historical guides. They should remain useful even after the project has moved on.
