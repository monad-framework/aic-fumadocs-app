# Inception

Inception asks whether there is a problem worth solving before architecture or implementation begins to dominate the conversation.

## Central questions

- What recurring engineering pain is fundamental rather than incidental?
- Who experiences it?
- Why are existing tools or workflows insufficient?
- What evidence would make the project uninteresting or unnecessary?
- What is the smallest capability that would still matter?
