Product definition translates domain understanding into a concrete answer to **what Monad should become**.

It establishes intended users, product boundaries, essential capabilities, non-goals, operating assumptions, and the smallest valuable form of the system.

## Read this phase through

</Cards>

## Boundary

Building Monad can explain why a product boundary was chosen, but the authoritative boundary must remain in Project, System, and accepted requirements.
