Architecture is where competing structural choices become explicit decisions with consequences.

This phase is especially important in Monad because the project depends on preserving distinctions that ordinary tooling often collapses: semantic identity versus native identity, capability versus authority, intent versus execution, response versus effect, evidence versus verification, and local state versus remote authority.
