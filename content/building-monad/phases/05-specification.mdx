# Specification

Specification makes architectural intent testable and implementable.

The emphasis shifts from "which structure should we choose?" to questions such as:

- What must the system guarantee?
- Which states and transitions are valid?
- What inputs and outputs are permitted?
- Which invariants are normative?
- What evidence will demonstrate conformance?
