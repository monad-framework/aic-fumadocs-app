The phase model is a **reading spine**, not a claim that complex engineering proceeds as a perfectly linear waterfall.

Monad can revisit discovery during implementation, revise architecture after verification, and re-enter specification when requirements change. The phases remain useful because they answer a different question:

> What kind of engineering concern is dominant in this part of the story?
