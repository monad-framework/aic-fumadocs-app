# AI-Native Engineering Operating System — System Architecture Specification

# 1. Purpose

This specification defines the logical system architecture of the **AI-Native Engineering Operating System (AIEOS)**.

It translates:

```text
AIEOS-FOUND-001
      ↓
AIEOS-REQ-001 v0.2.0
      ↓
AIEOS-DOM-001 v0.2.0
      ↓
Requirements Baseline RB-001
```

into an architecture capable of implementing the required engineering control system.

This specification determines:

* AIEOS system boundaries,
* logical components,
* component responsibilities,
* authority ownership,
* control flow,
* data flow,
* persistence responsibilities,
* event architecture,
* workflow coordination,
* agent orchestration boundaries,
* capability and authorization enforcement,
* verification and evidence handling,
* integration architecture,
* reconciliation,
* trust boundaries,
* failure semantics,
* local-first operation,
* extension architecture,
* MVP deployment shape.

This specification intentionally does not select:

* programming languages,
* application frameworks,
* database products,
* queue products,
* cloud platforms,
* model providers,
* source-control vendors,
* CI/CD vendors,
* UI frameworks,
* policy languages,
* container runtimes,
* workflow libraries.

Those decisions belong to `AIEOS-IMPL-001` and associated ADRs.

# 4. Architectural Style

AIEOS SHALL use a **governed control-plane architecture with ports-and-adapters boundaries**.

Conceptually:

```text
                   Humans
                     │
                     ▼
             ┌───────────────┐
             │ AIEOS Control │
             │     Plane     │
             └───────┬───────┘
                     │
       ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
       │             │             │
       ▼             ▼             ▼
   AI Agents    Automation      External
                                Systems
       │             │             │
       └─────────────┼─────────────┘
                     │
                     ▼
              Engineering Reality
```

The Control Plane SHALL govern engineering work.

It SHALL NOT require ownership of every external engineering artifact or system.

# 5. Architectural Principles

## ARCH-P-001 — Control is centralized logically, not necessarily physically

Governance decisions SHALL have a coherent authoritative model.

The implementation MAY distribute components physically.

---

## ARCH-P-002 — Native systems remain authoritative where appropriate

AIEOS SHALL integrate with native engineering systems rather than duplicating all of their behavior.

---

## ARCH-P-003 — Core governance state is explicit

Workflow state, policy decisions, Decisions, Operational Authorizations, and consequential provenance SHALL NOT exist solely as inferred external state.

---

## ARCH-P-004 — Agents are workers, not governors

AI agents SHALL perform reasoning and execution within boundaries established by deterministic governance.

They SHALL NOT own authoritative lifecycle progression.

---

## ARCH-P-005 — External systems are assumed fallible and asynchronous

AIEOS SHALL NOT depend upon distributed atomic transactions across unrelated external systems.

---

## ARCH-P-006 — Reconciliation is a first-class architectural function

Differences between AIEOS state and native system state SHALL be expected and governable.

---

## ARCH-P-007 — Evidence is externally observable

Verification architecture SHALL preserve Evidence independently from agent self-report.

---

## ARCH-P-008 — Authority is checked at action time

Protected actions SHALL be evaluated against current:

```text
Actor
Capability
Operational Authorization
Policy
Environment
Workflow State
Risk
```

rather than relying solely on earlier workflow approval.

---

## ARCH-P-009 — History is additive

Consequential historical records SHALL be append-oriented.

Corrections SHALL be represented rather than silently rewriting the past.

---

## ARCH-P-010 — Local operation is a first-class deployment mode

A useful AIEOS deployment SHALL be capable of operating locally without requiring a proprietary remote control service.

---

## ARCH-P-011 — Integration is replaceable

Vendor-specific behavior SHALL be isolated behind integration contracts.

---

## ARCH-P-012 — Start as a coherent system before becoming distributed

The MVP SHOULD favor a strongly bounded modular architecture over premature microservice decomposition.

Logical boundaries SHALL exist independently of deployment boundaries.

---

# 11. AIEOS Kernel

## 11.1 Responsibility

The **AIEOS Kernel** is the logical boundary containing authoritative AIEOS governance behavior.

It SHALL coordinate:

```text
commands
transactions
policy evaluation
state transitions
event creation
provenance
authorization checks
```

The Kernel SHALL NOT contain model-dependent reasoning.

---

## 11.2 Kernel invariants

The Kernel SHALL enforce:

```text
valid Subject identity

valid Workflow transition

required Policy satisfaction

required Operational Authorization

required Evidence presence

Decision authority

historical event creation

provenance capture
```

where applicable.

# 17. Policy Evaluation

Policy evaluation SHALL determine:

```text

```

Policy evaluation output SHALL be deterministic for the same authoritative inputs wherever the Policy semantics themselves are deterministic.

# 18. Semantic Authority Resolution

The governance architecture SHALL determine which source is authoritative for a governed Assertion.

Example:

```text
Question:
What is the current source code?

Authority:
Git repository

Question:
What is the accepted architecture decision?

Authority:
AIEOS Decision Artifact

Question:
Did CI pass?

Authority:
configured CI provider result
```

---

## 18.1 Authority conflicts

If applicable Authority Bindings conflict:

```text
Authority A
      ↘
     conflict
      ↗
Authority B
```

AIEOS SHALL surface an Authority Conflict.

Protected progression SHALL fail closed when the conflict affects mandatory governance.

---

# 19. Operational Authorization

Operational Authorization evaluation SHALL answer:

```text

 +
Target
 +
Environment
 +
Context
 +
Time

```

An Operational Authorization SHALL NOT itself provide technical access.

It authorizes the later provision or use of a Capability.

# 37. Transaction Boundary

Changes to authoritative AIEOS state that form one governance decision SHOULD commit atomically inside the authoritative persistence boundary.

For example:

```text
Transition accepted
+
Workflow State updated
+
Transition record
+
Event
+
Provenance
```

SHOULD either commit together or not commit.

---

# 38. External Side Effects

External system mutations SHALL NOT be assumed to participate in the same transaction as AIEOS state.

Example:

```text
AIEOS
  │
  ├── records intended GitHub operation
  │
  └── commits
         ↓
Connector performs GitHub operation
         ↓
result returned
         ↓
AIEOS records observed result
```

---

# 39. Side-Effect State Machine

Consequential external operations SHALL support explicit states such as:

```text
requested
authorized
dispatched
acknowledged
completed
failed
unknown
reconciliation-required
```

This prevents an external timeout from being treated as either definite success or definite failure without evidence.

---

# 40. Event Transport

Internal Events MAY be transported synchronously or asynchronously.

The architecture SHALL permit asynchronous subscribers for:

```text
projections
notifications
agent dispatch
integration operations
reconciliation
observability
analytics
```

Event transport technology is deferred.

---

# 41. Reliable Event Publication

Where Events trigger external or asynchronous effects, the implementation SHALL avoid a state in which:

```text
authoritative transaction succeeds

but

required event is silently lost
```

The implementation SHOULD use an equivalent of a **transactional outbox** or another atomic state/event publication pattern.

The exact mechanism belongs to implementation design.

---

# 67. Failure Philosophy

AIEOS SHALL distinguish:

```text
failure
unknown outcome
blocked condition
policy denial
authorization denial
external unavailability
stale information
conflict
```

These SHALL NOT be collapsed into a generic error state.

---

# 68. Agent Failure

If an Agent fails:

```text
Agent Execution
      ↓
failed / timed-out / cancelled
```

the Workflow SHALL NOT silently advance.

Retry SHALL be Policy-controlled.

---

# 69. AI Provider Failure

If an AI provider is unavailable:

* existing authoritative state SHALL remain valid;
* deterministic governance SHALL continue where possible;
* human operation SHALL remain possible where configured;
* Agent-dependent work MAY enter a blocked state;
* the system MAY route to another permitted provider.

AIEOS SHALL NOT corrupt Workflow State because AI reasoning is unavailable.

---

# 70. Integration Failure

When an external integration call fails, AIEOS SHALL record whether the external outcome is:

```text
known-failed

or

unknown
```

An ambiguous timeout SHALL NOT automatically be retried if the operation is not safely idempotent.

Reconciliation SHALL determine actual state.

---

# 71. Persistence Failure

A protected mutation SHALL NOT be reported as successful until authoritative AIEOS persistence succeeds.

If state cannot be persisted:

```text
operation result
=
failed or indeterminate
```

according to the external side effects already performed.

---

# 72. Policy Evaluation Failure

If mandatory Policy cannot be evaluated:

```text
Protected action
=
DENIED / BLOCKED
```

unless an explicit emergency Policy governs degraded operation.

Default behavior SHALL be fail-closed.

---

# 73. Semantic Authority Conflict

If authoritative sources conflict for information required by a protected transition:

```text
Transition
=
BLOCKED
```

until Policy or an authorized Decision resolves the conflict.

---

# 74. Evidence Staleness

Evidence invalidated by later state change SHALL NOT continue satisfying a mandatory gate unless applicable Policy permits it.

Example:

```text
Tests pass for commit A
        ↓
code changes to commit B
        ↓
Evidence for commit A
        =
not automatically valid for B
```

---

# 75. Recovery Architecture

AIEOS SHALL support restart-safe processing.

Long-running work SHOULD be representable as recoverable state rather than depending entirely on in-memory process state.

Examples:

```text

deployment
reconciliation operation
```


# 98. Architectural Invariants

## ARCH-INV-001 — One authoritative Workflow State

A Workflow Instance SHALL have one authoritative current Lifecycle State.

---

## ARCH-INV-002 — No direct agent lifecycle mutation

An Agent SHALL NOT directly set authoritative Workflow State.

---

## ARCH-INV-003 — No direct agent authority creation

An Agent SHALL NOT directly grant itself Operational Authorization or Capabilities.

---

## ARCH-INV-004 — Policy before protected action

Protected execution SHALL evaluate applicable Policy before execution.

---

## ARCH-INV-005 — Authorization before protected capability use

Protected Capability use SHALL require valid Operational Authorization where configured.

---

## ARCH-INV-006 — Evidence is external to agent assertion

Required Evidence SHALL exist independently from the Agent's statement that an operation succeeded.

---

## ARCH-INV-007 — External mutation is observable

Consequential external mutation SHALL have an explicit operation record and result state.

---

## ARCH-INV-008 — External uncertainty remains uncertainty

Unknown external outcomes SHALL NOT be rewritten as success or failure without evidence.

---

## ARCH-INV-009 — History is additive

Consequential historical state SHALL not be silently rewritten.

---

## ARCH-INV-010 — Projection is not authority

A derived projection SHALL NOT silently supersede its authoritative source.

---

## ARCH-INV-011 — Native system identity is not semantic identity

External native identifiers SHALL remain bindings unless explicitly adopted under identity policy.

---

## ARCH-INV-012 — Local operation does not depend on SaaS

A useful local AIEOS deployment SHALL not require a proprietary hosted AIEOS control service.

---
