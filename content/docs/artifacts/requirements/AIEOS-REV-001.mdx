# AI-Native Engineering Operating System — Requirements / Domain Reconciliation Review

# 1. Purpose

This review reconciles:

```text
AIEOS-FOUND-001
       ↓
AIEOS-REQ-001 v0.1.0
       ↕
AIEOS-DOM-001 v0.1.0
```

before establishment of the first accepted AIEOS requirements baseline.

The purpose of this review is to determine:

1. whether every required domain concept is supported by the requirements corpus;
2. whether every normative requirement has a coherent domain interpretation;
3. whether terminology is consistent;
4. whether conceptual boundaries are sufficiently precise;
5. whether requirements accidentally prescribe architecture;
6. whether the domain model accidentally prescribes implementation;
7. whether any requirements are missing;
8. whether any concepts are duplicated or contradictory;
9. whether authority, workflow, evidence, execution, and temporal semantics are coherent;
10. whether the requirements corpus is ready to become **Requirements Baseline RB-001**.

---

# 2. Review Inputs

The normative and supporting inputs are:

```text
AIEOS-FOUND-001
AI-Native Engineering Operating System
Foundation Specification

AIEOS-REQ-001 v0.1.0
System Requirements Specification

AIEOS-DOM-001 v0.1.0
Domain Model Specification
```

The principal reconciliation findings originate from `AIEOS-DOM-001 §67`.

The review also performs an independent cross-document inspection and identifies additional findings not explicitly recorded in that section.

---

# 3. Review Method

Each requirement and domain concept is evaluated against the following qualities:

```text
necessity
clarity
atomicity
semantic consistency
non-duplication
traceability
implementation neutrality
architecture neutrality where appropriate
testability
authority clarity
temporal clarity
identity clarity
lifecycle clarity
provenance completeness
```

Each finding receives one of the following dispositions:

```text
ACCEPT
    The finding is valid and requires change.

ACCEPT-WITH-MODIFICATION
    The concern is valid, but the proposed resolution is modified.

NO-CHANGE
    Existing requirements already adequately cover the concern.

DEFER
    The issue is valid but need not be resolved before RB-001.

REJECT
    The finding is not supported or would weaken the model.
```

Each finding is also classified as:

```text
BLOCKING
NON-BLOCKING
```

for establishment of `RB-001`.

---

# 4. Baseline Decision

## Decision

**AIEOS-REQ-001 v0.1.0 is NOT yet ready to become Requirements Baseline RB-001.**

The corpus is structurally sound and no fundamental contradiction has been discovered that invalidates the AIEOS operating model.

However, several semantic distinctions exposed by `AIEOS-DOM-001` need to become explicit requirements before the requirements corpus is frozen.

The required corrective sequence is:

```text
AIEOS-REQ-001 v0.1.0
        +
AIEOS-DOM-001 v0.1.0
        ↓
AIEOS-REV-001
        ↓
accepted reconciliation changes
        ↓
AIEOS-REQ-001 v0.2.0
AIEOS-DOM-001 v0.2.0
        ↓
cross-check
        ↓
Requirements Baseline RB-001
```

No architectural design should begin against `RB-001` until these changes are incorporated.

# 6. FIND-DOM-001 — Semantic Authority versus Operational Authorization

## Original concern

The current requirements use:

```text
REQ-AUTHORITY-*
```

for source-of-truth / semantic authority and:

```text
REQ-AUTH-*
```

for operational authorization.

Although logically distinct, the identifiers are unnecessarily similar.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT-WITH-MODIFICATION — BLOCKING**

The semantic distinction is foundational and should be made unmistakable before identifiers become baseline-stable.

## Decision

Retain:

```text
REQ-AUTHORITY-*
```

for **Semantic Authority**.

Rename:

```text
REQ-AUTH-*
```

to:

```text
REQ-AUTHZ-*
```

for **Operational Authorization**.

The corresponding terminology becomes:

```text
Semantic Authority
    =
Who or what establishes authoritative truth?

Operational Authorization
    =
Who or what may perform an action?
```

## Required modification

All references to requirements currently identified as:

```text
REQ-AUTH-001
...
REQ-AUTH-010
```

SHALL become:

```text
REQ-AUTHZ-001
...
REQ-AUTHZ-010
```

before `RB-001`.

Because the identifiers are not yet accepted, this is the appropriate time to make the change.

# 7. FIND-DOM-002 — Assertion Is Foundational

## Original concern

The domain model establishes **Assertion** as foundational to:

```text
semantic authority
conflict
evidence
relationships
inference
provenance
derived knowledge
validity
historical correction
```

but `AIEOS-REQ-001` contains no dedicated Assertion requirement family.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

Assertions are too central to remain implicit.

## Decision

Add:

```text
REQ-ASSERT
```

to the requirement taxonomy.

## Required requirements

### REQ-ASSERT-001 — Explicit assertion model

AIEOS MUST support explicitly represented propositions about governed Subjects where semantic governance requires proposition-level identity or status.

---

### REQ-ASSERT-002 — Assertion structure

An Assertion MUST be capable of identifying:

```text
subject
predicate or proposition type
value/object/content
status
origin
```

where applicable.

---

### REQ-ASSERT-003 — Assertion status

AIEOS MUST support assertion states equivalent to:

```text
proposed
inferred
accepted
disputed
rejected
superseded
invalid
```

where relevant.

---

### REQ-ASSERT-004 — Assertion provenance

Consequential Assertions MUST be capable of retaining provenance.

---

### REQ-ASSERT-005 — Assertion-level authority

Semantic Authority MUST be capable of applying at Assertion scope.

---

### REQ-ASSERT-006 — Uncertain propositions

AIEOS MUST support propositions whose truth has not yet been established.

Such propositions MUST remain distinguishable from accepted authoritative Assertions.

This requirement provides the basis for Hypothesis semantics.

---

### REQ-ASSERT-007 — Competing assertions

AIEOS MUST permit conflicting or competing Assertions to coexist while unresolved.

---

### REQ-ASSERT-008 — No automatic authority promotion

Generated, inferred, or imported Assertions MUST NOT become authoritative solely because they exist in the system.

---

# 8. FIND-DOM-003 — Workflow Definition versus Workflow Instance

## Original concern

`AIEOS-REQ-001` discusses workflows generally, while the domain model establishes a necessary distinction between:

```text
Workflow Definition
Workflow Instance
```

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

Without this distinction, workflow versioning and historical reconstruction are ambiguous.

## Required additions

### REQ-WF-014 — Workflow definition distinction

AIEOS MUST distinguish a versioned Workflow Definition from a concrete Workflow Instance.

---

### REQ-WF-015 — Workflow-instance binding

Each Workflow Instance MUST identify the Workflow Definition version governing it.

---

### REQ-WF-016 — Historical definition stability

Later modification of a Workflow Definition MUST NOT reinterpret historical transitions previously executed by existing Workflow Instances.

---

### REQ-WF-017 — Workflow-instance state

Authoritative lifecycle state MUST belong to a specific Workflow Instance.

---

# 9. FIND-DOM-004 — Approval versus Authorization

## Original concern

The current requirements use approval semantics operationally but do not establish **Approval** as distinct from Authorization.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT-WITH-MODIFICATION — BLOCKING**

The deeper issue is that **Decision** itself is under-specified.

Approval should therefore be modeled as a specialized Decision rather than receiving an isolated approval family.

## Decision

Add:

```text
REQ-DEC
```

for Decision and Approval semantics.

## Required requirements

### REQ-DEC-001 — Explicit decision model

AIEOS MUST support explicit governed Decisions.

---

### REQ-DEC-002 — Decision attribution

Every consequential Decision MUST identify the deciding Actor.

---

### REQ-DEC-003 — Decision authority

A consequential Decision MUST identify or derive the authority under which it was made.

---

### REQ-DEC-004 — Decision outcome

A Decision MUST represent the selected outcome or disposition.

---

### REQ-DEC-005 — Decision rationale

Policy MUST be capable of requiring rationale for a Decision.

---

### REQ-DEC-006 — Approval distinction

Approval MUST be represented as a Decision that a Subject, proposal, result, or transition is acceptable.

---

### REQ-DEC-007 — Approval is not authorization

Approval MUST NOT automatically imply Operational Authorization to perform a protected action unless an explicit Policy establishes that derivation.

---

### REQ-DEC-008 — Decision provenance

Consequential Decisions MUST retain provenance.

---

# 10. FIND-DOM-005 — Release versus Deployment

## Original concern

The requirements recognize Release and Deployment but do not explicitly state that they are distinct.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

## Required addition

### REQ-DEP-011 — Release/deployment distinction

AIEOS MUST distinguish:

```text
Release
=
a defined package or version of change intended for delivery

Deployment
=
the governed execution that applies a Release or Change to an Environment
```

A Release MUST NOT be considered deployed solely because it exists.

# 11. FIND-DOM-006 — Test Definition versus Test Execution

## Original concern

A Test is an Artifact.

Running the Test is an Execution.

The resulting output may become Evidence.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

This distinction is essential for reproducibility and evidence integrity.

## Required additions

### REQ-VER-011 — Test definition distinction

AIEOS MUST distinguish a Test definition from an Execution of that Test.

---

### REQ-VER-012 — Test execution evidence

A Test Execution MUST be capable of producing Evidence associated with:

```text
test identity
tested subject/state
execution environment
execution time
execution result
```

---

### REQ-VER-013 — Test result binding

A Test result MUST NOT be treated as applicable to a different governed state without an explicit validity basis.

---

# 12. FIND-DOM-007 — Temporal Semantics

## Original concern

`REQ-PROV-009` requires temporal ordering but does not explicitly distinguish:

```text
Recorded Time
Valid Time
```

## Disposition

**ACCEPT-WITH-MODIFICATION — BLOCKING**

Full bitemporal modeling is not required for every AIEOS Subject.

However, the semantic distinction is important enough that the platform must support it where applicable.

## Required additions

### REQ-PROV-011 — Recorded time

Consequential Events, Decisions, Authorizations, Assertions, Executions, and state transitions MUST support Recorded Time.

---

### REQ-PROV-012 — Applicable valid time

AIEOS MUST support Valid Time or equivalent effective-period semantics for domain concepts whose applicability can differ from their Recorded Time.

Examples include:

```text
Policy
Authorization
Authority Binding
Assertion
Exception
configuration
```

---

### REQ-PROV-013 — Historical correction

Correction of historical knowledge MUST preserve both:

```text
the originally recorded state

and

the later correction
```

where retention policy permits.

# 13. FIND-DOM-008 — Hypothesis Representation

## Original concern

Incident and research workflows require uncertain explanatory propositions without promoting them to established truth.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT-WITH-MODIFICATION — NON-BLOCKING**

A separate top-level Hypothesis requirement family is unnecessary.

A Hypothesis is best modeled as a specialized uncertain Assertion.

## Resolution

`REQ-ASSERT-006` SHALL provide the required normative basis.

`AIEOS-DOM-001` MAY retain **Hypothesis** as a named domain concept because the concept is operationally useful.

No `REQ-HYP-*` family is required at this stage.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — NON-BLOCKING**

## Required addition

### REQ-REC-008 — Explicit drift representation

# 16. FIND-REV-001 — Artifact Taxonomy Conflict

## Finding

`REQ-ART-002` currently states that the initial Artifact families include:

```text
Authorization
Observation
```

while `AIEOS-DOM-001` does not consistently model either as an Artifact.

The domain model also defines **Workflow Definition** as a versioned Artifact but does not include it in the main Artifact taxonomy.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

The requirements currently mix:

```text
Artifact types

and

other first-class governed records
```

## Decision

`REQ-ART-002` SHALL list actual core Artifact families only.

Recommended baseline:

```text
Intent
Requirement
Specification
Decision
Plan
Change
Test
Evaluation
Evidence
Review
Policy
Exception
Release
Incident
Workflow Definition
```

Authorization and Observation SHALL remain first-class governed domain concepts but SHALL NOT be required to be implemented as Artifact subtypes.

This preserves semantic flexibility.

# 17. FIND-REV-002 — Subject Identity Scope Is Too Narrow

## Finding

`REQ-ID-001` currently guarantees stable identity only for:

```text
governed work units
first-class engineering artifacts
```

The domain model requires stable identity for additional first-class Subjects such as:

```text
Actors
Workflow Instances
Executions
Deployments
Observations
Environments
```

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

## Required replacement

### REQ-ID-001 — Stable subject identity

Every first-class governed Subject MUST possess a stable semantic identity appropriate to its lifecycle and traceability requirements.

---

`REQ-ID-002` SHOULD likewise refer to **Subject identity** rather than only Artifact identity.

Recommended revision:

### REQ-ID-002 — Location-independent identity

The semantic identity of a governed Subject MUST NOT depend exclusively upon a mutable representation or physical location such as:

```text
filename
repository path
issue number
URL
database row position
temporary execution location
infrastructure address
```

# 18. FIND-REV-003 — Work Unit Lifecycle Ambiguity

## Finding

`REQ-WU-003` currently states:

> Every work unit MUST have an explicit lifecycle status.

The domain model establishes authoritative lifecycle state as belonging to a **Workflow Instance**.

These are not necessarily the same concept.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

## Decision

Separate:

```text
Work Unit coordination status

from

Workflow Instance lifecycle state
```

## Required replacement

### REQ-WU-003 — Work-unit coordination status

A Work Unit MUST expose an explicit coordination status sufficient to communicate its current engineering condition.

That status MAY be derived from one or more governing Workflow Instances.

It MUST NOT replace or ambiguously overwrite the authoritative Lifecycle State of a Workflow Instance.

---

This leaves open whether future architectures permit multiple concurrent Workflow Instances for one Work Unit.

That question remains non-blocking.

---

# 19. FIND-REV-004 — Control-Plane Wording Leaks Architecture

## Finding

`REQ-SYS-006` currently uses the title:

> Deterministic control plane

and `REQ-WF-005` explicitly refers to:

> the workflow engine

These terms imply architectural components before `AIEOS-ARCH-001`.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

The requirements should describe required behavior, not component partitioning.

## Required revisions

### REQ-SYS-006 — Deterministic governance

Authoritative lifecycle state changes and policy-enforced governance decisions MUST be determined through deterministic system rules rather than unverified model judgment alone.

---

### REQ-WF-005 — Agent transition recommendation

Agents MAY recommend state transitions.

AIEOS MUST independently determine whether a proposed transition is permissible under the applicable Workflow Definition, Policy, Evidence, Risk, and Authorization state.

---

Terms such as:

```text
Workflow Engine
Policy Engine
Authority Engine
Control Plane
Capability Broker
```

remain valid architectural candidates but SHALL be defined by `AIEOS-ARCH-001`, not assumed by the requirements baseline.

# 23. FIND-REV-008 — Decision Is Under-Specified

## Finding

Decision is included as an Artifact but lacks a dedicated requirements family.

This becomes problematic because:

```text
approval
risk acceptance
policy exception
architecture choice
intent acceptance
```

are all forms of governed Decision.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — BLOCKING**

The new `REQ-DEC` requirements defined in Section 9 SHALL be introduced before `RB-001`.

This change also clarifies that a Decision is not simply a human UI action.

Agents MAY propose Decisions.

Authority determines which Decisions can become accepted.

# 24. FIND-REV-009 — Event Semantics Are Not Explicit Enough

## Finding

The domain model defines **Event** as an immutable historical record of something consequential occurring.

The requirements corpus discusses events and audit history but does not require an explicit event semantic model.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — NON-BLOCKING**

AIEOS need not be implemented as an event-sourced architecture.

However, historical consequential events must be representable.

## Required addition

### REQ-PROV-014 — Consequential event representation

AIEOS MUST be capable of representing consequential historical occurrences as immutable or append-only historical records whose meaning is not silently rewritten.

This requirement SHALL NOT mandate event sourcing.

---

# 25. FIND-REV-010 — Representation Role versus Semantic Authority

## Finding

`AIEOS-DOM-001` permits Representation roles such as:

```text
authoritative
derived
generated
projection
cached
```

while also correctly stating that Representation role alone does not establish Semantic Authority.

The use of the word `authoritative` as a Representation role risks confusion.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT — NON-BLOCKING**

## Decision

Change the recommended Representation role vocabulary from:

```text
authoritative
```

to:

```text
authority-bearing
```

or preferably:

```text
source
```

while preserving explicit Semantic Authority separately.

Recommended roles:

```text
source
derived
generated
projection
cached
coordination
transient
external
```

A source Representation may be associated with Semantic Authority through an Authority Binding.

It does not acquire authority merely from the role label.

# 26. Additional Terminology Decisions

The following terminology is accepted for all subsequent AIEOS documents.

## 26.1 Semantic Authority

Use:

> **Semantic Authority**

for authority to establish what is authoritative or believed true.

Do not shorten this to merely `Authority` where ambiguity with operational permission is possible.

---

## 26.2 Operational Authorization

Use:

> **Operational Authorization**

for permission to perform a governed action.

Requirement family:

```text
REQ-AUTHZ
```

---

## 26.3 Approval

Use:

> **Approval**

for a Decision that something is acceptable.

Approval is not Operational Authorization.

---

## 26.4 Capability

Use:

> **Capability**

for technical ability.

Capability is not Operational Authorization.

---

## 26.5 Workflow Definition

Use:

> **Workflow Definition**

for the versioned description of permitted workflow behavior.

---

## 26.6 Workflow Instance

Use:

> **Workflow Instance**

for a concrete application of a Workflow Definition to governed work.

---

## 26.7 Lifecycle State

Lifecycle State belongs to a Workflow Instance.

---

## 26.8 Work Unit status

Work Unit status is a coordination-level view and may be derived from workflow state.

---

## 26.9 Evidence

Evidence is an observable basis for an Assertion.

It is not equivalent to the Assertion.

---

## 26.10 Verification

Verification determines whether defined conditions hold.

---

## 26.11 Review

Review performs broader assessment.

---

## 26.12 Decision

Decision establishes an authoritative choice when made by an appropriate authority.
