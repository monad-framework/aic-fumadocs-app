# Engineering Journal

This section is the chronological engineering record for Monad.

Unlike the authoritative documentation under `/docs`, journal entries capture work as it happens: investigations, implementation notes, experiments, problems encountered, decisions in progress, and lessons learned.

The journal may describe ideas that later change. When a journal entry conflicts with an accepted specification, requirement, decision record, or current system documentation, the authoritative artifact takes precedence.
